How Ollama & Qwen 2.5 Power Po Second Brain

Ollama acts as the local model server (http://localhost:11434) hosting Qwen 2.5 7B — an advanced 7.61-billion-parameter Large Language Model pretrained on 18 trillion tokens. Unlike standard chatbots that forget your data, Po Second Brain injects a live snapshot of your personal SQLite database into Qwen 2.5’s 32,768-token context window on every query.

When you issue a command, Qwen 2.5 both answers your question and emits structured [ACTION: {...}] payloads that automatically schedule calendar milestones, log diary entries, record sleep/calorie data, and store long-term memories.
